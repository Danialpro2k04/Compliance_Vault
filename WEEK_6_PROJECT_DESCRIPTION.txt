1. PROJECT OVERVIEW & CONTEXT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TradeGuard is an autonomous, governed AI system for international trade compliance
and freight documentation. Across Weeks 1 to 5, the core pipeline components were
built:
  • Week 1 & 2: Freight document extraction (Azure AI Document Intelligence) & cross-doc validation
  • Week 3: Sanctions screening engine (SanctionScope) using Qdrant vector similarity & fuzzy matching
  • Week 4: HS tariff classification agent (TariffAI) using RAG + Gemini LLM
  • Week 5: TradeGuard Core Pipeline (LangGraph state machine connecting all engines with HITL)

Week 6 addresses the fundamental governance challenge in regulated industries:
"In trade compliance, the AI's answer isn't enough. You need to prove HOW it
arrived at that answer, WHO reviewed it, and that NOBODY tampered with the record
after the fact."

ComplianceVault is the governance layer that wraps around TradeGuard Core,
providing mathematical tamper-evidence, full traceability, role-based access control,
authorized human overrides, and regulatory-ready PDF reporting.

2. COMPLIANCE & REGULATORY STANDARDS IMPLEMENTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This project is engineered directly to meet international AI governance standards:

1. EU AI ACT (REGULATION (EU) 2024/1689):
   • Article 14 (Human Oversight): Implemented via "Compliance Officer Override"
     and the human review flow. High-risk systems must allow human operators to
     remain aware of AI capabilities, monitor operation, and override or reverse
     the output. Written justifications are mandatory and recorded immutably.
   • Article 50 (Transparency Obligations): Complete visibility into the AI's
     reasoning chain (Document Intake -> Entity Extraction -> Sanctions Screening
     -> HS Tariff Classification -> Risk Assessment -> Final Decision).

2. ISO/IEC 42001 (AI MANAGEMENT SYSTEM):
   • Section on Auditability & Traceability: Full hash-chained logs recording every
     input, output, confidence score, timestamp, model verdict, and human interaction.

3. CORE ARCHITECTURAL COMPONENTS BUILT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. HASH-CHAINED TAMPER-EVIDENT AUDIT STORE (`app/audit_store.py`):
   • Cryptographic SHA-256 chain where:
       Entry 0: Hash = SHA256("" + Entry_0_JSON)
       Entry N: Hash = SHA256(Entry_{N-1}.Hash + Entry_N_JSON)
   • If a single character, timestamp, or score in any past entry is modified,
     every subsequent hash in the chain immediately breaks.
   • Supports dual-level verification:
       - Document-level audit chain (per-shipment decisions)
       - System governance log (tracking logins, exports, overrides, and verifications)
   • JSONL append-only persistence to prevent accidental or malicious overwriting.

2. ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL (RBAC) (`app/rbac.py` & `app/config.py`):
   • Operator:
       - Permissions: view_documents, submit_documents, view_own_reports
       - Intended for logistics clerks & freight forwarding staff
   • Compliance Officer:
       - Permissions: view_all_reports, review_flagged, export_reports
       - Intended for certified compliance officers reviewing YELLOW shipments
   • Administrator:
       - Permissions: override_decision, manage_users, view_audit_chain, verify_integrity
       - Intended for system administrators and compliance heads

3. COMPLIANCE OFFICER OVERRIDE (`app/document_store.py`):
   • Allows authorized administrators to overturn an AI decision (e.g., clearing a
     false-positive sanctions match or rejecting an ambiguous HS tariff code).
   • Mandatory justification requirement (minimum 20 characters).
   • The override is treated as a first-class event:
       - Appended and hash-chained into the document's audit log
       - Logged into the central governance audit log
       - Rendered with full historical comparison (previous vs new decision)

4. INTERACTIVE WEB DASHBOARD (`app/main.py`, `app/templates/`, `app/static/`):
   • Responsive UI built with FastAPI + Jinja2 + CSS3 + Vanilla JavaScript:
       - Statistics cards: Total processed, Approved, Blocked, Awaiting Review, Overridden
       - Real-time audit chain integrity indicators (valid / broken counter)
       - Risk distribution chart (GREEN / YELLOW / RED)
       - Searchable and filterable table (by status, risk level, date range)
       - Full document drill-down page showing party details, routing, sanctions matches,
         HS reasoning tree (Chapter -> Heading -> Subheading), and audit trail.

5. REGULATORY PDF REPORT EXPORTER (`app/pdf_exporter.py`):
   • Built using `fpdf2` with TradeGuard enterprise branding.
   • Generates multi-page compliance reports featuring:
       1. Document Summary & Risk Level
       2. Trade Parties & Route
       3. Sanctions Screening Detail (matched names, sources, match scores)
       4. HS Tariff Classification & Reasoning Chain
       5. Document Discrepancies & Flagged Risk Factors
       6. Human Review & Override History
       7. Complete Hash-Chained Audit Trail with SHA-256 hashes
       8. Legal regulatory declaration and export metadata timestamp

8. LINKEDIN POST DRAFT (WEEK 6)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In trade compliance, an AI model's output is practically worthless without proof.

If an AI flags or clears an international cargo container, customs authorities
and banks don't just ask "what was the decision?"
They demand:
1. HOW did the system arrive at that conclusion?
2. WHO reviewed and authorized the decision?
3. Can you prove NOBODY modified the records after the fact?

Week 6 of building TradeGuard: Built ComplianceVault — the governance and audit
layer that turns autonomous agent workflows into legally defensible compliance systems.

Here is what we shipped this weekend:

🔒 Tamper-Evident Hash Chains: Every AI action, document intake, sanctions check,
and tariff classification is recorded in an append-only ledger using SHA-256 hash
chaining. If anyone tampers with a single timestamp or confidence score in the past,
every subsequent block in the chain invalidates immediately.

👤 Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Dedicated privilege boundaries separating
Logistics Operators, Compliance Officers, and Administrators.

⚡ Compliance Officer Override (EU AI Act Article 14): Human oversight isn't a
checkbox. Authorized compliance heads can override AI decisions, but it requires
mandatory written legal justification that is permanently chained into the immutable log.

📄 Regulatory PDF Exporter: Full audit trail dossiers generated in seconds for
customs and regulatory submission (EU AI Act Article 50 & ISO/IEC 42001 aligned).

AI in high-stakes enterprise isn't about replacing humans — it's about governed,
auditable, and verifiable collaboration between AI agents and certified officers.
